Sustainable construction has moved from marketing language to measurable practice. Here's what that looks like on a real project.

## Lifecycle thinking

We evaluate materials on their whole-life impact — not just the up-front cost, but energy, maintenance, and end-of-life recovery.

## Efficient site logistics

Smarter scheduling and local sourcing cut both emissions and delays.

## Designing for the long term

Buildings designed for adaptability last longer and waste less — the greenest building is the one you never have to tear down.
