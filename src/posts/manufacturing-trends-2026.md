The manufacturing landscape is changing faster than at any point in the last century. Here are the shifts our engineers are watching most closely.

## Smart automation everywhere

Collaborative robots and sensor-rich production lines are no longer reserved for the largest plants. Mid-sized operations now deploy them to improve throughput and safety at once.

## Sustainable by design

Material choices, energy recovery, and waste reduction are becoming core design constraints rather than afterthoughts — and customers increasingly expect it.

## Data-driven maintenance

Predictive maintenance uses live equipment data to catch failures before they happen, cutting unplanned downtime dramatically.

> The factories that win the next decade will be the ones that treat data as a raw material.
