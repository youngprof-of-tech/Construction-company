Efficiency isn't a single project — it's a set of habits applied consistently. These five strategies deliver the biggest returns.

## 1. Standardize before you automate

Automating a messy process just makes the mess faster. Document and stabilize first.

## 2. Measure what matters

Track a small number of meaningful metrics rather than drowning teams in dashboards.

## 3. Invest in preventive maintenance

Every hour of planned maintenance saves several hours of emergency repair.

## 4. Train for safety continuously

A strong safety culture reduces incidents and protects your most valuable asset — your people.

## 5. Close the feedback loop

The people closest to the work usually know where the friction is. Give them a channel to fix it.
